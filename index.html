🎯 CS tips

How to play

  1. Pick a mode, type your nick and press Play.
  2. Click or tap the chicken. Normal and Strafe: body = 1 egg, head = 3 golden eggs.
  3. Precision: kill = 1, one-shot kill = 5, headshot = 2, one-shot headshot = 7. If she escapes, zero. Missing before you hit ruins the bonus.
  4. Every click that misses counts as a wasted shot and lowers your accuracy.
  5. If the chicken escapes, your streak resets. You get 60 seconds and she only speeds up.
